# First-Response SLA Review

**To:** Neha Kulkarni, Support Operations Manager  
**Subject:** Weekly SLA breaches and a focused next step

Across **11,200 valid tickets from January 2025 through June 2026**, **21.79%** missed their channel-specific first-response target. The stated goal of 15% is a reduction of **6.79 percentage points**.

The highest breach rate was on the **Morning shift: 2,018 of 6,264 tickets (32.22%)**. For comparison, Day was 393 of 4,668 (8.42%) and Night was 29 of 268 (10.82%). Morning is the clearest place to investigate first, but these figures do not establish that a shift or an individual caused the breaches. Ticket volume, channel mix, and case mix should be considered before drawing performance conclusions.

## Estimated business impact

At the planning assumptions of **650 tickets per week** and **Rs 350 per breached ticket**, reducing the pooled historical rate from 21.79% to 15% is estimated to save about **Rs 200,688 per quarter**. This is a scenario, not confirmed credits or guaranteed savings; actual volume and credit eligibility may differ.

## Recommended next step

Run a four-week review of Morning-shift queue load and routing, especially for chat. Review weekly breach counts alongside ticket volume and channel mix, then test a routing or coverage adjustment before considering staffing changes. Use the agent-level report to identify cases for review, not as a standalone ranking.

## Important caveat

The report passed a 50-ticket internal consistency sample with no mismatches, but that is not an independent accuracy audit. Near-target response times and invalid negative durations still need additional quality checks before these results are used for formal performance or financial decisions.
